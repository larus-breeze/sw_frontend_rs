\section{\tr{configuration}}
\subsection{\tr{Polare glider}}

\tr{To obtain the correct speed-to-fly information, you must set the correct polar values for your glider type. The LARUS Vario display comes factory-set with more than 200 polars from various gliders. Select the correct or closest polar from the list. After selecting the glider type, you should update the empty weight data for your glider. If you cannot find your glider type in the list, you can select any glider polar and change the individual settings to the values for your glider polar. The name of the glider cannot be changed.}


\picnote
    {pictograph-yellow-warning.pdf} {\tr{Each time the glider list is called up in the polar settings, all previously changed values are replaced by the hard-coded values of the selected glider.}}
\picnote
    {pictograph-yellow-warning.pdf} {\tr{Ensure that the maximum water ballast matches the information provided by XCSoar, otherwise the water ballast calibration with XSCoar will not function correctly.}}


\subsection{\tr{Calibration of the Larus Senor Box}}

\tr{Before you begin your first flight, the position sensors of the LARUS sensor box must be precisely adjusted. The calibration steps are carried out using a simple procedure, which is described below and initiated via functions in the LARUS Vario display. Calibration is carried out in two stages.}

\subsubsection{\tr{Initial calibration on the ground:}}

\tr{Assemble your glider and place it on a flat surface. After you have taken up the individual positions, wait until you can no longer feel any vibrations in the aircraft before continuing with the calibration. Do not use a tail trolley to fix the vertical axis of your glider during the following procedures.}

\begin{itemize}
    \item   \tr{Lower the left wing, wait briefly, and then call up the Settings / Sensor Box / Left Wing Down function.}
    \item   \tr{Remove the right wing, wait a moment, and then call up the Settings / Sensor Box / Right Wing Down function.}
    \item   \tr{Hold the wing horizontally, wait, then call up the function "Settings / Sensor box / Wing straight".}
    \item   \tr{For this step, it is important to complete all three of the steps mentioned above. The order of the steps does not matter, but they must be completed in full. Call up the Settings / Sensor box / Calculate alignment function.}
\end{itemize}


\subsubsection{\tr{Fine adjustment in the air:}}

\tr{The pitch angle calibration is performed during flight. It is recommended that you perform this step during a flight that is not disturbed by thermal gusts. Align your glider at the speed with the best glide ratio (if you have flaps, set them to this speed). Go to "Settings / Sensor box / Straight flight". That's it. You can check the calibration by switching to the AHRS display.}
