\section{\hr{Configuration}}
\subsection{\hr{Glider Polar}}

\tr{To obtain the correct speed-to-fly information, you must set the correctpolarvalues for your glider type. The LARUS Vario display comes factory-equipped with
more than 200 polarfrom various gliders. Select the correct or closestpolar from the list. After selecting the glider type, you should update the empty weight data for your 
glider.

If you cannot find your glider type in the list, you can select any
gliderpolar and change the individual settings to the values for your
gliderpolar. The name of the glider cannot be changed.}

\subsection{\hr{Calibration of the Larus Senor Box}}

\tr{Before you begin your first flight, the position sensors of the LARUS sensor unit must be precisely adjusted.
The calibration steps are carried out using a simple procedure, which is described below and initiated via functions in the LARUS Vario display.
Calibration is carried out in two stages.
1. Calibration of the pitch and roll sensors}

\subsubsection{\hr{Initial Calibration on the Ground:}}

\tr{Assemble your glider and place it on a flat surface. After you have taken up the
individual positions, wait until there are no more vibrations in the aircraft
before continuing with the calibration. Do not use a tail trolley to fix the vertical
axis of your glider during the following procedures.}

\begin{itemize}
    \item   \tr{Lower the left wing, wait a moment, and call up the function
            Settings / Sensor Unit / Lower Left Wing.}
    \item   \tr{Remove the right wing, wait a moment, and call up the function
            Settings / Sensor unit / Right wing down.}
    \item   \tr{Keep the sash horizontal, wait, call up the function "Settings
            / Sensor unit / Sash straight".}
    \item   \tr{For this step, it is important to complete all three of the steps mentioned above.
            The order of the steps does not matter, but they must be completed in full.
            Call up the function Settings / Sensor unit / Calculate alignment.
            The alignment is calculated automatically.}
\end{itemize}


\subsubsection{\hr{Fine Adjustment in the Air:}}

\tr{The pitch angle calibration is performed during flight. It is recommended that you perform this
 step during a flight that is not disturbed by thermal gusts. Align
 your glider at the speed with the best glide ratio (if you have flaps,
 set it to this speed). Go to "Settings / Sensor Unit /
 Straight Flight". That's it. You can check the calibration by switching to the
 AHRS display.}
