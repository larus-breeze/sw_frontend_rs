\subsection{Safety}

\subsubsection{Safety \tr{precautions}}
\picnote
	{pictograph-yellow-warning.pdf} {\tr{Duty to provide information: Every person involved in the installation or operation of LARUS components must read and observe the safety-related sections of these operating instructions.}}

\subsubsection{\tr{Proper use}} \tr{The LARUS Vario Display visualises data measured and calculated by the LARUS sensor box. LARUS was developed to quickly and reliably calculate the direction and strength of thermals and wind. To do this, the sensor unit combines data from high-precision sensors and GNSS receivers in sophisticated algorithms. The LARUS Vario
display is installed in the instrument panel. The LARUS sensor box provides glider pilots with accurate information about wind, vertical air movements and the aircraft's flight attitude. Its use is limited to daytime VFR conditions. Safety decisions must be made independently of the installation and operation of LARUS components.}

\subsubsection{\tr{Improper use}}

\tr{Incorrect use will invalidate all liability and warranty claims. Incorrect use is defined as any use that deviates from the above-mentioned purposes, in particular:}

\begin{itemize}
	\item 	\tr{The use of LARUS data under non-VFR conditions or at night is prohibited. LARUS is not certified for this purpose. Although LARUS provides AHRS data to XCSoar, you should not rely on the artificial horizon display.}
	\item 	\tr{Use of LARUS data during aerobatics or under flight conditions with high angles of attack (stall) or high G-forces. The algorithm was designed for normal flight conditions.}
	\item 	\tr{Operation of the LARUS Vario Display outside the conditions defined in the technical data, such as high or low supply voltage, excessive temperatures or humidity.}
\end{itemize}
